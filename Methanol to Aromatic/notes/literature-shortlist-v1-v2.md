# Part II — Shortlist V2

## Scope of V2
V2 emphasizes:
- **roughly last 5 years**
- **higher-quality / higher-impact journals preferred**
- **stronger direct relevance to methanol-to-aromatics / aromatic selectivity / zeolite-controlled aromatization**

Important note:
Pure “methanol to aromatics” title matches are not extremely numerous in the last 5 years.
So V2 also includes highly relevant papers framed through:
- methanol-to-hydrocarbon selectivity
- zeolite-controlled aromatics formation
- methanol-mediated CO2-to-aromatics bridging logic

---

## A. Highest-priority recent papers

### 1. `10.1002/anie.202114786`
**Rational Design of Zinc/Zeolite Catalyst: Selective Formation of p-Xylene from Methanol to Aromatics Reaction**
- **Year:** 2022
- **Journal:** Angewandte Chemie International Edition
- **Type:** original research
- **Priority:** highest
- **Why it matters:** highly direct methanol-to-aromatics paper; especially valuable because it focuses on **p-xylene selectivity**, which makes it excellent for later extraction of catalyst–structure–selectivity relationships.
- **Best use:** key primary paper for MTA catalyst and selectivity analysis

### 2. `10.1002/aoc.5932`
**Highly shape-selective Zn-P/HZSM-5 zeolite catalyst for methanol conversion to light aromatics**
- **Year:** 2020
- **Journal:** Applied Organometallic Chemistry
- **Type:** original research
- **Priority:** high
- **Why it matters:** directly relevant to Zn-modified HZSM-5, shape selectivity, and light-aromatic formation; very useful as a catalyst-system extraction target.
- **Best use:** zeolite modification / aromatic selectivity benchmark

### 3. `10.1039/d4cs01042f`
**Selectivity control by zeolites during methanol-mediated CO2 hydrogenation processes**
- **Year:** 2025
- **Journal:** Chemical Society Reviews
- **Type:** review
- **Priority:** highest
- **Why it matters:** not purely “methanol to aromatics”, but extremely important as a **bridge paper** connecting methanol-mediated routes, zeolite selectivity, and CO2-to-hydrocarbon/aromatics logic.
- **Best use:** conceptual bridge between the Methanol-to-Aromatic project and the CO2-to-Aromatic project
