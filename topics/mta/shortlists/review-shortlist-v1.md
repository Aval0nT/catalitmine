# Methanol to Aromatic — Review shortlist v1

## Priority core reviews / context papers

1. `10.1038/s41929-018-0078-5`
   - Recent trends and fundamental insights in the methanol-to-hydrocarbons process
   - Very strong context review for MTH/MTA chemistry and mechanism space.

2. `10.1016/j.ccr.2022.214982`
   - Direct hydrogenation of carbon dioxide to value-added aromatics
   - Directly aligned with CO2-to-aromatics direction.

3. `10.1080/01614940.2022.2099058`
   - Direct conversion of CO2 into aromatics over multifunctional heterogeneous catalysts
   - Directly aligned with CO2-to-aromatics route design.

4. `10.1016/j.jcou.2022.101969`
   - Zeolite-based catalyst for direct conversion of CO2 to C2+ hydrocarbon: A review
   - Broader than aromatics but useful for zeolite/multifunctional catalyst context.

5. `10.1039/d2cs00456a`
   - Advanced zeolite and ordered mesoporous silica-based catalysts for the conversion of CO2 to chemicals and fuels
   - Useful support/zeolite design review.

6. `10.1007/s13203-016-0156-z`
   - Advances in the study of coke formation over zeolite catalysts in the methanol-to-hydrocarbon process
   - Important for MTA/MTH deactivation understanding.

## Working decision
Primary project focus for now:
- `Methanol to Aromatic`

Secondary parallel context lane:
- `CO2 to Aromatic`

Reason:
- Methanol-to-aromatic is mechanistically closer to immediate aromatic formation over zeolite systems and should provide a cleaner first literature map.
- CO2-to-aromatic can then be framed as an upstream + tandem / multifunctional extension.
